You can delete your TechBlob account and its data yourself.

On the site

Sign in, open Account, tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account, comments, submissions, reports and blocks are removed right away and you are signed out.

By email

If you can't sign in, email info@kavarollc.com from your account's email with the subject "Delete my TechBlob account". We confirm when it's done.
