If you believe something on TechBlob infringes your copyright, send a notice to Kavaro LLC at info@kavarollc.com with the subject "DMCA notice".

What a notice must include

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • The copyrighted work you say is infringed.
  • The TechBlob page (its link) with the material you want removed.
  • Your name, address, phone number and email.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized by the owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

What happens next

We remove or disable the material promptly and tell the person who posted it. Videos that play through YouTube's embed are also YouTube's to remove; we take the TechBlob page down either way.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed by mistake, email info@kavarollc.com with your signature, the removed material and where it was, a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake or misidentification, your name, address and phone, and consent to the jurisdiction of your local federal court. We restore it in 10 to 14 business days unless the claimant files suit.

Repeat infringers

Accounts that receive repeated valid copyright notices are closed.
